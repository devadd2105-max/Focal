How Focal works

What it measures

Focal shrinks your photo and measures it with ordinary image maths. There is no AI model and no server.

  • Light. A histogram, the median brightness against middle grey, and how many pixels are pure white or pure black.
  • Sharpness. How steep the strongest edges are, with the noise floor taken off so grain is not mistaken for detail.
  • The subject. A saliency map: areas that stand out in colour, brightness and sharpness. The biggest cluster is treated as your subject.
  • Lines. A Hough transform finds long straight lines. The flattest strong one is the horizon, and lines that meet give a vanishing point.
  • Shape and colour. Mirror symmetry, empty areas, repeating patterns, dominant colours and colour casts.
  • Camera settings. Aperture, shutter speed, ISO and more are read from the JPEG's EXIF data, or typed in by you, then checked against what the pixels show.
Measured or estimated?

Every finding is labelled. Measured means it comes straight from the pixels, such as the histogram or horizon angle. Estimate means a rule of thumb that can be wrong, such as "foreground layer" or "frame within a frame". Treat estimates as prompts to look again, not verdicts.

What your goal changes

Composition rules are guidelines. When you describe your goal, Focal re-weights each finding, and marks some "faults" as deliberate: crushed shadows in a silhouette, a tilt in street work, empty space in minimalism. It reads your words by matching keywords against a list of goals, so it shows which goals it matched. Technical problems such as blur or heavy noise are still reported.

What Focal cannot judge

Focal cannot judge emotion, storytelling, originality, S-curves, juxtaposition or scale. A photo can score low and still be a good photograph. Use the score as a checklist, not a grade of your eye.

Privacy

Your photo is decoded and analysed in this browser tab. It is never sent anywhere, and Focal keeps no copy. Closing the tab removes it. Straighten and crop work on a copy in memory, and the download is saved on your own device.
