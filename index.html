How it worksSource

A photo coach that works on the picture itself.

Drop in a photo. Focal checks the framing, light, sharpness, colour and camera settings, then draws what to fix right on the photo.

Your photo never leaves this page. It is analysed inside your browser and is not uploaded or stored.

Say what the photo is for. Focal then judges it against your goal, so a deliberate dark silhouette is not marked as a mistake.
